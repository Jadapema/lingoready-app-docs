# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 3, 2026**

This policy explains what Lingoready ("we", "us") collects, why, and what control you have. The short version, in plain words:

- **Your audio is deleted right after scoring**, unless you turn on retention.
- **You can export or delete your data anytime** from Profile.
- **We never sell your data** or share recordings with employers.

## 1. What we collect

**Account & profile.** Email, name (optional), country, native language, learning goal, profession, CEFR level estimate, reminder time, app settings. Collected when you sign up and edit your profile.

**Practice content.** Transcripts of what you say in sessions, drills and assessments; texts you submit for writing review; words you save; the AI feedback generated for you.

**Voice audio.** Processed to transcribe and score your speech. By default audio is **deleted right after processing** — a daily job also purges any stragglers. If you enable *"Keep my voice recordings"* in Profile, recordings are retained so you can replay them; you can turn this off at any time.

**Usage & device data.** App events (screens viewed, sessions started, drill scores) via Firebase Analytics; crash reports via Sentry; a push-notification token if you allow notifications; approximate usage metering (speaking minutes, AI cost accounting).

**Billing.** Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat. We receive your plan and entitlement status — **never your card details**.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the product: transcribe, score, and coach your speaking; sync your progress across devices.
- To enforce plan limits (monthly speaking minutes) and prevent abuse.
- To send the notifications you asked for (practice reminders, "your report is ready").
- To fix crashes and improve features using aggregated analytics.

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not share your recordings or transcripts with employers, recruiters, or advertisers.

## 3. AI processors

Your speech and texts are processed by these providers, under contracts limiting use to providing the service to us:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| OpenAI | Speech-to-text, coaching feedback (LLM), text-to-speech |
| Deepgram | Live speech-to-text during sessions |
| Microsoft Azure | Pronunciation assessment |
| Google Firebase | Authentication, analytics, push notifications |
| Sentry | Crash reporting |
| RevenueCat | Subscription entitlements |

We do not permit these providers to use your content to train their models where an opt-out is available.

## 4. Retention

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Voice audio | Deleted after processing (default) or until you disable retention |
| Transcripts & feedback | While your account is active |
| Account profile | While your account is active |
| Deleted accounts | Soft-deleted immediately, hard-purged within 30 days |

## 5. Your rights

From **Profile → Privacy & data** in the app you can:

- **Export my data** — download everything we hold about you (profile, transcripts, feedback, words, writing reviews) as JSON;
- **Delete account** — removes your account immediately and permanently purges data within 30 days;
- toggle **voice retention** on or off.

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may also have rights to correction, restriction and portability — write to us and we'll honor them.

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Authentication is delegated to Firebase; API access requires a verified identity token. Production access is limited to the operator of the service.

## 7. Children

Lingoready is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the app and the effective date updated.

Privacy questions or requests: **support@lingoready.app**
