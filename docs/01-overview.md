# Overview

## What Lingoready is

Lingoready is a **voice-first AI English coach for working professionals**. Users rehearse the exact conversations that decide their careers — technical interviews, standups, client calls, presentations — out loud, with an AI coach in character, and get honest, specific feedback in seconds.

**Positioning:** not a vocabulary app. The unit of value is a *rehearsed real moment* plus *two concrete fixes* that make the user sound more senior at work.

## Target user

Non-native professionals (B1–B2 typical) who:
- are interviewing for English-speaking / remote roles, or
- work in international teams and want to speak up with confidence.

Primary personas by field: software & tech, product/design, marketing, finance/consulting, sales, health, academia (onboarding profiles all eight).

## Core loops

1. **Hook (first session):** free 4-minute spoken level check → CEFR badge + skill radar → "your plan to B2". No account wall before value.
2. **Daily loop:** open app → today's recommended scenario (tuned to profession + goal) → 5–12 min live session → feedback with 2 fixes → suggested words into the word bank.
3. **Reinforcement loop:** weak skills surface as 2–5 min drills; mastered words rotate out; streak + weekly minutes keep cadence.
4. **Monetization:** free tier (3 lessons/path, 20 speaking min/month) → Pro trial at the moment of highest motivation (assessment results / locked lesson).

## Design language

Navy `#1F3A5F` (authority) · Blue `#2E75B6` (action) · Amber `#E8A33D` (energy/CTAs) · Inter typeface · 12–16px radii · full dark-mode parity. Practice/assessment flows use a dark "call" aesthetic; management screens are light and calm.
